Example Agency Announces New Research Program

Example Agency, September 20, 2026

The Example Agency today announced a new research program aimed at demonstrating fixture-based parsing. The program will run for three years and fund up to twelve performers across two technical areas.

Technical Area 1 focuses on deterministic extraction of main content from server-rendered HTML. Technical Area 2 focuses on provenance: every extracted field must be traceable to the bytes it came from.

Proposals are due November 1, 2026. See the program page and the broad agency announcement (PDF).
